CONCURRENT
==========

Everyone writes their orders in secret, and everyone's orders happen at once.


STARTING IT

1. Right-click the zip and choose "Extract All", then open the folder it makes.
   (Started from inside the zip, Windows stops to ask about extracting first.)

2. Double-click CONCURRENT.exe. It's the whole game in one file: nothing to install.

3. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (the game isn't signed by
   a big company). Click "More info", then "Run anyway". It only asks once.

4. On the title screen, click "First time? Play the Tutorial". The advisor walks you
   through a short war against Captain Oren, one idea a round.

5. Then "New Game". The Known World against Captain Oren and Governor Quill is a good
   first war.


HANDY KEYS

  Space ........ roll your dice in a battle, and move on
  Esc .......... close a menu, or skip the rest of the battles
  F11 .......... full screen
  L ............ the map as 3D land, or as a flat chart
  Rules coin ... How to Play, any time (bottom right)


ON A LAPTOP

  Words too small?  Settings, then Text size: Large. F11 fills the whole screen.
  Slow or jerky?    Press L for the flat chart. The battles can stay on the land, or go
                    flat too: Settings, then "The land in 3D".


Your war saves itself after every round: "Continue" on the title screen picks it up.
Starting a new war (or the tutorial) ends the saved one, so the game asks first.
Every war you play is also kept, finished or not, in a folder called "wars"
(paste %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\CONCURRENT\wars into the File Explorer bar).
Alex may ask you to zip it and send it: it helps balance the game.
Sound too loud or too quiet? Settings, on the title screen or under the Menu coin.
