CONCURRENT
==========

Everyone writes their orders in secret, and everyone's orders happen at once.


STARTING IT

1. Double-click CONCURRENT.exe. It's the whole game in one file: nothing to install.

2. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (the game isn't signed by
   a big company). Click "More info", then "Run anyway". It only asks once.

3. On the title screen, click "First time? Play the Tutorial". The advisor walks you
   through a short war against Captain Oren, one idea a round.

4. Then "New Game". The Known World against Captain Oren and Governor Quill is a good
   first war.


HANDY KEYS

  Space ........ roll your dice in a battle, and move on
  Esc .......... close a menu, or skip the rest of the battles
  F11 .......... full screen
  L ............ the map as 3D land, or as a flat chart
  Rules coin ... How to Play, any time (bottom right)


Your war saves itself after every round: "Continue" on the title screen picks it up.
Sound too loud or too quiet? Settings, on the title screen or under the Menu coin.
